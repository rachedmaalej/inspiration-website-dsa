# What to share, when, and with whom

## The core structure: 2 meetings, not 1

The documents map onto two distinct conversations. Conflating them in one meeting overwhelms and dilutes both.

| Meeting | Trigger | Documents |
|---|---|---|
| M1 — Direction artistique | Before March 15 | `competitive-landscape-dsa.html` + `mobile-proposals.html` (companion for Q-05/Q-06) |
| M2 — Proposition & Go/NoGo | After 8 decisions locked | `proposition-site-web-dsa.html` → then `devis-phase1-site-web.html` |

---

## Document-by-document decision

### Share in M1 (artistic direction)

**`competitive-landscape-dsa.html`** — already planned, FigJam board built from it. The primary vehicle for the 8 decisions.

**`mobile-proposals.html`** — share as a companion, not the main event. Open it after Q-05 (typography) and Q-06 (hero) come up in FigJam. Concrete mockups make abstract choices tangible. Don't lead with it.

### Share in M2 (proposal)

**`proposition-site-web-dsa.html`** — this IS the inflection point. The Go/NoGo lives here. Structure the meeting around it: walk through the approach, the 3 phases, the rationale. Ask for a decision at the end.

**`devis-phase1-site-web.html`** — share after they've said yes to the approach, not before. The order matters: understand the value first, then see the price. If you lead with the devis, the conversation becomes about cost instead of vision.

### Share after Go decision

**`dsa-master-timeline.html`** — useful for Elyssa and Lilia to see the full multi-year picture, but only once they've committed to Phase 1. Before the Go decision it creates scope anxiety ("we're signing up for all of this?"). After the Go decision it creates confidence ("we have a plan for everything").

### Never share with Elyssa/Lilia

**`dsa-website-action-plan.html`** — developer-facing sprint plan. Too granular. Would generate questions about implementation details instead of strategic decisions.

**`dsa-design-proposals.html`** — internal Kanban dashboard. Not their layer of the project.

---

## The proposal meeting (M2) flow

Once the 8 decisions are locked from M1:

1. **Recap the 8 decisions (5 min)** — "voici ce qu'on a décidé ensemble"
2. **Open `proposition-site-web-dsa.html`**
   - Walk through the vertical slices rationale
   - Show Phase 1 scope clearly (what ships in May)
   - Explicit Go/NoGo ask at the end
3. **If Go → open `devis-phase1-site-web.html`**
   - Line items make sense because they've already bought into the scope
4. **Agree on start date (April 7) and next checkpoint**

The proposal document already contains the "Tranches Verticales" explainer with the analogy — let it do the persuasion work, don't paraphrase it.
